SEC. 02 — Venture Formats

Cross-marketing, engineered.

Every alliance is structured — not improvised. Four proven formats govern how brands inside the lattice move together.

Reciprocal Referral Corridors

FMT — A

Structured two-way client flow: our members route to partner FBOs, charter desks, and insurers; partners route maintenance and management demand back to the command desk. Attribution tracked, value balanced quarterly.

Co-Branded Capability Programs

FMT — B

Joint service packages marketed under both names — e.g., "FBO × Dawns Talon arrival-to-airworthy" programs where a landing aircraft receives detailing, inspection, and concierge turnaround as one seamless product.

Event & Demonstration Circuits

FMT — C

Co-hosted hangar events, autonomous-inspection demonstrations, and OEM showcase days at Lowcountry facilities — shared audience, shared cost, doubled reach in the regional aviation community.

Content & Authority Exchange

FMT — D

Technical briefings, joint white papers, and cross-published case studies that build domain authority for both brands — positioning the corridor itself as the subject worth reading about.

SEC. 03 — Entry Protocol

How alliances form.

Admission to the lattice is deliberate and bilateral. We evaluate partners the way clients evaluate us — on standards, stability, and the discipline to honor a handshake at scale.

Apply as Infrastructure Partner→
  1. 01

    Channel Opened

    Submit through the intake portal as an Infrastructure Partner, or write to the operations desk directly. Include your facility class, certificates held, and the venture format you envision.

  2. 02

    Mutual Vetting

    We audit certificates, insurance posture, and reputation; you audit ours. Both parties must clear the bar before any logo touches a page.

  3. 03

    Charter Drafted

    A one-page alliance charter defines scope, attribution, referral economics, brand usage, and exit terms — signed by principals, not departments.

  4. 04

    Formation Activated

    Your mark joins this page, referrals begin flowing, and the first joint venture is scheduled within one quarter — momentum is the contract.
