SEC. 03 — Fiscal Framework

Structured for the write-off.

501(c)(3) Proxy Routing

Dawns Talon coordinates equipment donations through established 501(c)(3) workforce-development and educational partners. Donors receive formal acknowledgment letters from the qualified charitable entity — the documentation standard your tax counsel expects.

Corporate Equipment Programs

Flight departments refreshing tooling, drone programs decommissioning fleets, and manufacturers clearing demo inventory can convert depreciation dead-ends into deductible contributions — with logistics handled entirely by our desk.

Documentation Discipline

Every transfer is logged with chain-of-custody records, condition assessments, and fair-market valuation support. Items valued over $5,000 are flagged for the qualified appraisal IRS Form 8283 requires.

Dawns Talon provides logistics and coordination, not tax advice. Donors should confirm deductibility with a licensed CPA or tax counsel.
